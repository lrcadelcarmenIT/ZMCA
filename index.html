06 · Common questions

Before you inquire.

What information should I prepare?

Start with the product, current process, desired output, available floor space, power requirements if known, package format, and target timeline. Photos or short videos of the current process are useful.

Can ZMCA help if I do not know the machine name?

Yes. Describe what you are producing and the result you need. ZMCA can help identify the relevant equipment category before discussing a specific configuration.

Do you handle both processing and packaging?

Yes. Inquiries can cover one production stage, one packaging requirement, or an initial plan for a complete line.

Are specifications and prices shown online?

Exact specifications and pricing depend on the product, capacity, configuration, shipping, and installation requirements. A consultation prevents a misleading one-size-fits-all quote.
